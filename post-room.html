Why post your room here?

This page is useful for room owners, tenants, PG managers, hostel providers, and roommates who want to publish available accommodation details in a simple format. By adding complete information, your listing becomes easier to understand and more useful for interested visitors.

What details improve your listing?

Good listings usually include room type, real rent amount, deposit, available date, nearby location, facilities, and original photos. Adding a correct Google Maps link also helps people check the area before contacting you.

How to post correctly

>Fill your real name, phone, and email address. >Select the correct room type and gender preference. >Mention all important facilities clearly. >Add rent-per-person values if sharing options are available. >Upload actual photos of the room or property. >Use an accurate location and Google Maps link.

Photo and trust tips

>Upload bright and original room photos. >Avoid fake, copied, or unrelated images. >Keep rent and deposit details transparent. >Do not provide misleading room information. >Update your listing if room availability changes. >Help visitors by using a real and working map URL.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use this room posting form?

Owners, tenants, roommates, PG operators, and hostel managers can use this page to post accommodation details.

Can I upload multiple room photos?

Yes, this form supports multiple room photos so users can better understand the property before contacting you.

Why should I add facilities clearly?

Facilities such as AC, WiFi, food, parking, laundry, and security help users quickly compare listings and contact the right property owner.

Why is Google Maps link important?

A valid Google Maps link makes the location easier to verify and helps people understand nearby landmarks or travel convenience.
